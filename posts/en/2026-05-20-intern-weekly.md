# My Engineering Internship Weekly Template

A simple structure I use every week:

1. **Inputs**: tasks, requirements, context.
2. **Process**: what I did and why.
3. **Problems**: blockers, failures, unknowns.
4. **Retrospective**: what worked / what did not.
5. **Next actions**: concrete tasks for next week.

This keeps weekly updates actionable and easy to review.
