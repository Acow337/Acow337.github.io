# CS336 Lecture 2: Resource Accounting

> Goal: turn training cost into quick, actionable formulas.

## 1. What problem are we solving?

Two core engineering questions:

1. **Compute accounting**: given model size, tokens, and GPUs, how long will training take?
2. **Memory accounting**: with fixed VRAM and optimizer setup, how large can the model be?

$$
\text{Estimate first, optimize second.}
$$

## 2. Unified tensor view

Most training objects are tensors:

- Parameters
- Activations
- Gradients
- Optimizer states
- Batch data

So resource accounting is mainly:

$$
\text{FLOPs} \quad \text{and} \quad \text{Bytes}
$$

## 3. Compute accounting

For decoder-only Transformers, a common approximation is:

$$
\text{FLOPs/token} \approx 6N
$$

With total tokens $T$:

$$
\text{Total FLOPs} \approx 6NT
$$

Let:

- $G$: number of GPUs
- $P$: per-GPU peak throughput (FLOPs/s)
- $\eta$: effective utilization

Then:

$$
\text{Throughput} \approx G \cdot P \cdot \eta
$$

$$
\text{Time} \approx \frac{6NT}{G \cdot P \cdot \eta}
$$

## 4. Memory accounting

A practical decomposition:

$$
M_{\text{total}} = M_{\text{params}} + M_{\text{grads}} + M_{\text{optim}} + M_{\text{acts}} + M_{\text{misc}}
$$

For AdamW, per-parameter memory intuition:

$$
\text{Bytes/param} \approx b_w + b_g + b_m + b_v
$$

## 5. Practical workflow

1. Define target: $N$, $T$.
2. Compute total FLOPs with $6NT$.
3. Estimate throughput with $GP\eta$.
4. Estimate wall-clock time.
5. Validate VRAM budget including activation peaks.

## TL;DR

- FLOPs: $6NT$
- Time: $\frac{6NT}{GP\eta}$
- Memory: params + grads + optim + activations + misc
